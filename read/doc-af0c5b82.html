FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT. Northfield Bio and all data below are invented for demonstration.

Methods. Formulations F1, F2, F3 and F6 were placed in dialysis cassettes at 37 °C in phosphate buffer, and semaglutide release was measured by HPLC for 21 days.
Diabetic rats (n=6 per group) received a single subcutaneous injection of F3 or daily injections of semaglutide solution.

Results. F3 released 9% of semaglutide in the first 24 hours and 82% by day 21.
A single injection of F3 kept plasma semaglutide above the efficacy threshold for 21 days, with blood glucose comparable to daily injections.
F6 (alginate in place of hyaluronate) released 14% in the first 24 hours, so other anionic polysaccharides may also work, but F6 was not tested in animals.

Limitations. Only rats were studied. No human data exist. Stability beyond 4 weeks at 2-8 °C has not been measured.
