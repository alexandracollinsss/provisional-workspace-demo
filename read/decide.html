What goes in this filing

Included groups: formulation, process, presentation, example.

  • thermo-responsive gel carrier (Essential) — 18% w/w poloxamer 407 with 0.5% w/w sodium hyaluronate and 0.1 mM zinc chloride (F3)
  • semaglutide cargo (Essential) — 2 mg/mL semaglutide
  • poloxamer 407 at 18% w/w (Essential) — 18% w/w poloxamer 407; 16% was too soft and 20% gelled in the needle
  • sodium hyaluronate (Essential) — 0.5% w/w sodium hyaluronate
  • zinc chloride (Unresolved question) — 0.1 mM zinc chloride; never tested without zinc
  • sodium alginate variant (Alternative version) — 0.5% w/w sodium alginate (F6), in vitro only
  • chitosan variant (Tested, did not work) — 0.5% w/w chitosan aggregated (F5)
  • preparing cold and adding drug last (Essential) — Polymer dissolved at 4 °C overnight; semaglutide added last below 8 °C
  • setting under the skin (Optional) — Gels within 30 seconds at body temperature
  • prefilled glass syringe (Optional) — Glass syringes stored at 2-8 °C
  • 21-day rat study (Essential) — Single F3 injection, 21 days above threshold in diabetic rats (n=6)
  • low burst release (Essential) — 9% in 24 hours, 82% by day 21

Still unresolved

  • zinc chloride: 0.1 mM zinc chloride; never tested without zinc
  • Open question: Besides hyaluronate and alginate, which polymers or polysaccharides were tried, and did any fail?
  • Open question: Is zinc chloride needed, or does hyaluronate alone give the slow release?
