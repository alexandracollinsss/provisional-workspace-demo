Preparation protocol PP-03 (fictional)

founder

FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT. Northfield Bio and all data below are invented for demonstration.

1. Disperse poloxamer 407 in cold phosphate buffer at 4 °C and stir overnight until clear.
2. Add sodium hyaluronate and stir at 4 °C for 2 hours.
3. Add zinc chloride, then add semaglutide last while keeping the mixture below 8 °C.
4. Fill into glass syringes at 4 °C and store at 2-8 °C.
5. At room temperature the liquid stays injectable for about 10 minutes; under the skin it sets into a gel within 30 seconds.
