Provisional Workspace: read-only demo

A tool that helps a scientific lead at a small pharma company turn new formulation results into a source-linked provisional patent application draft for patent counsel to review, before an external milestone.

All company data here is fictional (Northfield Bio does not exist). The marketed drug (FDA Orange Book) and comparison patents are real public records. This snapshot is static: no live edits, no AI calls.
